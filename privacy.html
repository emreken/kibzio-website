Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nuvyro Studio ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in Kibzio ("the App"), an Android application for image compression, resizing, and conversion.

1. Image Processing and Privacy

Kibzio processes images locally on your device. Your selected images are not uploaded to servers operated by Nuvyro Studio for compression, resizing, or conversion.

The App accesses images you select to perform requested operations. Processed files may be saved to your device or shared using Android features at your direction.

If you choose to share an image through another application or service, that recipient may process the image under its own privacy policy.

2. Information Processed by the App

Kibzio may process image files, image metadata, processing preferences, and locally stored application settings to provide its features.

Local application data may include preferences, processing history, presets, and information needed to maintain access to purchased features.

Nuvyro Studio does not require you to create a Kibzio account to use the App.

3. Advertising

The free version of Kibzio uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location derived from network information, ad interactions, and technical diagnostic information, subject to applicable settings, consent, and law.

This information may be used for advertising, fraud prevention, measurement, and related purposes. Personalized advertising is subject to applicable consent requirements.

Where required, the App will request consent or provide applicable privacy choices before using advertising technologies.

Google privacy information: Google Privacy Policy .

Advertising technology information: How Google uses advertising information .

4. Pro Purchases and Subscriptions

Kibzio offers optional paid features through Google Play Billing, including a recurring Monthly Pro subscription and a one-time Lifetime Pro purchase.

Purchases and payments are processed by Google Play. Nuvyro Studio does not directly collect or store your payment card details.

The App may locally store purchase entitlement information to determine access to Pro features, including during temporary periods without an internet connection.

Purchase-related information may be processed by Google under its own privacy practices.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Images and processed outputs are stored locally on your device or in locations you select. You control files saved on your device.

Application preferences, processing history, and locally cached purchase information may remain on your device until removed through application features, Android settings, or uninstallation, subject to Android's storage and backup behavior.

Third-party services such as Google AdMob and Google Play may retain information according to their own policies.

6. Data Sharing

Nuvyro Studio does not sell your selected images or upload them to its own servers for image processing.

Advertising and purchase-related information may be processed by Google and relevant service providers as described above. Images may also be shared with other services when you explicitly initiate sharing.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to design Kibzio with privacy and security in mind, including local image processing. However, no software or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Kibzio is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children. Advertising and privacy requirements applicable to children will be handled according to applicable laws and platform policies.

9. Your Choices and Rights

You can manage locally saved images and application data using Kibzio features and Android settings. You may also manage Google advertising preferences and Google Play subscriptions through Google's services.

Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights, including rights relating to access, deletion, or objection to certain processing. Contact us for privacy-related requests.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to Kibzio, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements. The updated version will be published here with a revised effective date.
