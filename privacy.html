Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026 · Last updated: November 21, 2026

Nuvyro Studio ("we", "us", or "our") provides Kibzio ("Kibzio" or the "App"), an Android application for image compression, resizing, and format conversion. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you use the App, including its free, advertising-supported version and optional Pro features.

1. Local Image Processing

Kibzio is designed to process selected images on your Android device. Images selected for compression, resizing, or conversion are not uploaded to servers operated by Nuvyro Studio to perform those operations. The App may read image content and metadata, such as dimensions, format, orientation, and file size, as necessary to provide the requested operation.

Processed images may be saved to your device or a location you choose. If you explicitly share an image using Android's sharing features, the receiving application or service handles that image under its own terms and privacy practices.

Local image processing does not mean that no other information is processed by third parties: advertising and Google Play services are described separately below.

2. Information Used to Operate Kibzio

The App may store settings, processing preferences, presets, local processing history, and purchase entitlement information on your device. The App may access images or folders that you select through Android's file and media selection features, subject to permissions and access granted by Android.

You do not need to create a Kibzio account to use the App. Nuvyro Studio does not operate an account server or a server for uploading your images for processing.

3. Advertising in the Free Version

The free version of Kibzio is designed to display advertisements provided through Google AdMob (Google). AdMob and, where applicable, its advertising partners may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from network information;
  • device and app information, operating system information, and advertising identifiers where available and permitted;
  • ad impressions, interactions, performance, and fraud-prevention signals; and
  • diagnostic and technical information relevant to advertising services.

Depending on your region, consent, device settings, and applicable law, this information may be used to deliver contextual or personalized advertisements, measure advertising performance, limit repeated ads, and prevent abuse. The categories of information actually processed depend on the Google advertising technologies and settings in use.

Monthly Pro and Lifetime Pro are designed to provide an ad-free experience in Kibzio. Google Play services may still process purchase-related information independently of whether ads are displayed.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy, Google's advertising technologies information, and Google's guidance on AdMob and user privacy.

4. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, Kibzio will request advertising-related consent before enabling processing that requires consent. The choices offered may vary by country or region. Refusing consent does not prevent you from using the App's core image processing functions; advertising may be limited or non-personalized, or may not be shown, depending on applicable requirements and available ad serving options.

Where a privacy-options entry is legally required, Kibzio will provide a way to review or change applicable advertising privacy choices in the App. You can also manage certain advertising preferences through your Android and Google account settings. Withdrawal of consent does not retroactively undo processing that occurred lawfully before withdrawal.

For privacy requests or assistance locating available choices, contact kibziophoto@gmail.com.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

Kibzio offers optional Monthly Pro subscriptions and Lifetime Pro one-time purchases through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles the payment process. We do not directly receive or store your full payment card number.

The App checks Google Play purchase ownership and may store limited entitlement status locally to provide access to purchased features, including during temporary offline periods. Subscription management, cancellation, refunds, and payment-related data processing are also governed by Google Play's applicable terms and policies.

For details, see Google Play Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

6. Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Images and generated output files remain in storage locations on your device that you or Android select. You can delete these files using Kibzio or Android's file management features, as applicable. Local app settings, history, presets, and entitlement cache may persist until you clear app data, remove the relevant items, or uninstall the App, subject to Android's storage and backup behavior. Uninstalling the App does not necessarily delete exported images saved outside its private storage.

Google and its advertising or payment service providers may retain information under their own retention policies. Nuvyro Studio cannot directly delete information held independently by Google, but you can use Google's privacy controls or contact Google for requests concerning its services.

To ask about personal information, deletion, or other privacy rights relating to information we control, email kibziophoto@gmail.com. We will review requests in accordance with applicable law and explain where a request concerns information controlled by another provider.

7. Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell your selected images. Kibzio does not transmit images to Nuvyro Studio servers for image processing. Information used for advertising may be processed by Google AdMob and relevant advertising partners; purchase information is processed through Google Play. Images are shared with other applications or services only when you choose to export or share them through supported device features.

Third-party providers maintain their own privacy practices, security measures, and data retention policies.

8. Security

We aim to limit unnecessary access to your images and rely on Android's application sandboxing, media access controls, and device storage protections. No application or electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for the security of your device and any images you choose to share with third parties.

9. Children's Privacy

Kibzio is a general-purpose image utility and is not specifically designed for or directed at children. We do not knowingly request that children submit personal information directly to Nuvyro Studio through an in-app account or image-upload service. Advertising shown in the App will be configured in accordance with applicable laws, age-related requirements, and Google Play policies for the audiences to which the App is made available.

If you believe a child's personal information has been handled inappropriately by us, contact kibziophoto@gmail.com.

10. International Processing and Your Rights

Third-party providers such as Google may process advertising and purchase-related information in countries other than your own, subject to their policies and applicable safeguards. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of certain personal information, or to object to certain processing or withdraw consent where applicable.

Contact kibziophoto@gmail.com to exercise rights relating to processing for which Nuvyro Studio is responsible. Rights and available remedies depend on applicable law.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App, advertising services, legal requirements, or our privacy practices. We will publish the revised version on this page and update the date above. Where legally required, we will provide additional notice or request renewed consent.
